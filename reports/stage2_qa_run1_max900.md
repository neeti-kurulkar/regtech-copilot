## Example answers

**A06. A borrower wants to shift their loan to another lender. How long does the NBFC have to give its consent or objection?**

```text
According to para 19 of the Responsible Business Conduct Directions, 2025, in case of receipt of a request from a borrower for the transfer of a borrowal account, the consent or objection of the NBFC must be conveyed "within 21 days from the date of receipt of request" [1][2]. Such a transfer shall be as per transparent contractual terms in consonance with law [1][2].

Sources:
    [1] Responsible Business Conduct Directions, 2025 | A. Fair Practices Code for NBFCs > A.3 Disbursement of loans including changes in terms and conditions | paras 17-20
    [2] Responsible Business Conduct Directions, 2025 | A. Fair Practices Code for NBFCs > A.4 General | paras 19-21
```

**A19. Which borrowers must an NBFC report to CRILC, and how often?**

```text
An NBFC must report credit information, including the classification of an account as SMA, to the Central Repository of Information on Large Credits (CRILC) on a monthly basis for all borrowers having an aggregate exposure of ₹5 crore and above with them, as per para 20 of the Resolution of Stressed Assets Directions [1]. 

Additionally, under para 21 of the Resolution of Stressed Assets Directions, an NBFC must submit a weekly report of instances of default by all borrowers with an aggregate exposure of ₹5 crore and above by close of business on every Friday, or the preceding working day if Friday happens to be a holiday [1].

Sources:
    [1] Resolution of Stressed Assets Directions, 2025 | Chapter II - Definitions and General Requirements > C. Early identification and reporting of stress | paras 19-22
```
